GitHandler is free software under **GPL-3.0-or-later** and it has a single owner: one person decides
the licence, the roadmap and what gets merged. Contributions are welcome, and this agreement is what
makes them compatible with that setup.

Read it once, sign it once: it then covers every contribution you submit to this repository.

The project takes issues only for now, so this agreement lies dormant: it applies from the first day
the maintainer merges external code. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

    documentation, a translation, an image or any other material.
-   **The maintainer** is the owner of this repository, currently the GitHub account

If your employer owns the rights to what you produce at work, either you are authorised to sign on its
behalf or its rights over the contribution are waived. Otherwise do not sign.

## 6. How you accept

On your first pull request the maintainer asks you to sign. You accept by posting this comment on the
pull request:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The link to that comment is recorded in [`docs/cla-signatures.md`](docs/cla-signatures.md) and covers
every contribution you submit afterwards. If the pull request is rejected or you withdraw it, the
contribution stays yours and this agreement grants nothing.
